Privacy

Layrly changes the layout of pages you open on X and Instagram. The extension processes the visible page on your device. Text cards and the notification backdrop are generated in memory.

Local settings

Chrome local storage holds your layout, theme and display preferences. Resetting preferences in Settings clears those choices. Layrly has no analytics, account system or backend.

Browser access

The extension uses activeTab to identify the site when you open the toolbar popup, and storage to remember your choices. Its content scripts run on the HTTPS versions of x.com, twitter.com and instagram.com, including their www hosts.

Platform activity

Likes, replies, notifications and media use the platform’s own controls and network connections. Your activity on those services remains subject to their policies.
